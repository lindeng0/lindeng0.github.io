My research broadly examines how people interact with artificial intelligence and how AI-enabled systems shape judgment, learning, and behavior.

## Current Research Themes

### Human–AI Co-creation and AI Disclosure
I study how different forms of AI involvement and disclosure influence people's evaluation of human–AI co-created work and subsequent decision making.

### Generative AI in Education
I examine how the communication style and design of generative-AI tutors can influence student motivation, engagement, learning, and perceptions of autonomy support.

### Digital Innovation and Data-driven Decision Making
I am interested in how organizations and users adopt emerging digital technologies and create value from data and AI.

## Selected Technical Projects

**Image Captioning System**  
Developed an encoder–decoder image captioning system with attention using a pretrained ResNet-50 and LSTM, and deployed the model through a Flask web application.

**Cathay Pacific – CUHK Practicum: Photolog System**  
Trained and deployed YOLOv8 models for aircraft cargo-latch inspection and developed a Flask/MySQL web application supporting image-based inspection workflows.

**Little Red Book Crawler App**  
Developed a Python-based data collection application for retrieving public-facing social-media data for research purposes.

**NFL Game Outcome Analysis**  
Built and evaluated multiple predictive models using NFL Big Data Bowl and betting data to study factors associated with game outcomes.
